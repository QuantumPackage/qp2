=========
dressing
=========

Dressing of the Hamiltonian used in the |CIPSI| iteration.

The dressing adds to the one-body Hamiltonian a two-body operator
built from the current wave function, so that the Hamiltonian
diagonalized in the selected space is a better approximation of the
full Hamiltonian than the bare one. The subroutine
`run_dressing` builds the dressed wave function iteratively until the
convergence criteria `thresh_dressed_ci` and `n_it_max_dressed_ci`
are met, and the energy :math:`E_0` in the denominator of the
dressing is provided by `energy.irp.f`.

The construction of the dressing operators is parallelized over the
|ZeroMQ| slave processes: `dress_zmq.irp.f` drives the slaves,
`dress_slave.irp.f` and `run_dress_slave.irp.f` run on the slaves,
and the single and double excitation operators used in the dressing
are generated by `alpha_factory.irp.f`. The stochastic variant of the
dressing is in `dress_stoch_routines.irp.f`.
 
 
 
EZFIO parameters 
---------------- 
 
.. option:: thresh_dressed_ci
 
    Threshold on the convergence of the dressed |CI| energy
 
    Default: 1.e-5
 
.. option:: n_it_max_dressed_ci
 
    Maximum number of dressed |CI| iterations
 
    Default: 10
 
.. option:: dress_relative_error
 
    Stop stochastic dressing when the relative error is smaller than :option:`perturbation PT2_relative_error`
 
    Default: 0.001
